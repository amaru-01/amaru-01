## Direction contract

THESIS: A whole practice raised from one line: every page is a flat sheet ruled by a single sweeping charcoal crease, and the work is set as numbered plates along it. Refuses the developer-portfolio default of a hero over a grid of equal cards, and the glass template it replaces.

OWN-WORLD: Cream cotton paper (#F2EFE6) as the ground everywhere, soft-black crease ink (#1C1C1C), graphite shadow greys (#6A6A6A, #A8A8A8, #D6D6D6), highlight #FBFAF6; dark scheme is graphite paper with cream ink. Inclined high-contrast serif italic (Bodoni Moda) for display, labels and figure numbers; Source Serif 4 roman for reading text. Buttons are arched strips (curved top edge), cards are hairline-ruled plates, figures numbered like plates (Pl. 1). No glass, no blobs, no gradient text, no pills.

STORY: The visitor reads Paul's name and role, sees the engineering work as plates with stack and links, notices the visual practice as range, and emails him or opens GitHub.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left half: name in large italic display, one-line role, arched primary "See the work" and secondary "Email me". Right half: the authored SVG paper shell rising from the arc that sweeps from top centre across the sheet. Bottom band ruled by a curved hairline: three plates, selected code (top three projects as a list), the principle (one line on design + engineering), and selected visual work (featured image or the T.C.O.A link).

FORM: Curved Crease Shell (catalog paper-folds-pleats-deployable-curved-crease-shell), user-adopted challenger, not on my grounded list; seed:f688354f98c93168. Signature interaction: the crease arc draws in once on load and the shell's fold deepens as the visitor scrolls the first screen; reduced motion renders the settled state.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
